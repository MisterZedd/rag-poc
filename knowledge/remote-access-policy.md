# Cascadia Office of Technology — Remote Access Policy

Document ID: COT-NET-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Office of the Chief Information Security Officer

## Requirements
- Remote access to state systems requires connection through the state VPN service.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for every VPN session.
- Split tunneling is prohibited; all traffic must route through the state network while connected.

## Session Limits
- VPN sessions automatically disconnect after 12 hours and must be re-established.
- Idle sessions are disconnected after 30 minutes of inactivity.

## Devices
- Only state-managed and compliant devices may establish a VPN connection.
- Personal devices may access email through the approved mobile application but may not connect to the VPN.
