# Cascadia Office of Technology — Technology Procurement Policy

Document ID: COT-FIN-008
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Office of the Chief Financial Officer

## Approval Thresholds
- Purchases under $25,000 may be approved by a division director.
- Purchases of $25,000 or more require a documented sole-source justification if not competitively bid.
- Purchases over $50,000 require approval from the Chief Technology Officer.
- Purchases over $250,000 require approval from the state procurement board.

## Vendor Requirements
- All technology vendors must complete a security and data-handling assessment before contract award.
- Cloud and AI vendors must document data residency and confirm that state data is not used to train shared models.
