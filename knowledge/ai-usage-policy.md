# Cascadia Office of Technology — Artificial Intelligence Usage Policy

Document ID: COT-GOV-007
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Owner: Office of the Chief Technology Officer

## Approved Tools
- The state-licensed M365 Copilot is approved for use with Internal and Restricted data, because it operates within the state's Azure tenant and does not use state data to train models.
- Enterprise Azure OpenAI deployments within the state tenant are approved for building state applications.

## Prohibited Use
- Confidential data must never be entered into any AI tool, including approved tools.
- Public consumer AI tools (free chatbots outside the state tenant) must not receive any Internal, Restricted, or Confidential data.

## Requirements for AI Systems
- Any AI system that generates responses for state business must ground its answers in approved source data and must cite sources where feasible.
- AI systems must be evaluated for accuracy before deployment, and re-evaluated after significant changes.
- Data classification must be verified before any dataset is used with an AI system.
