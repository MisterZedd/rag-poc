# Cascadia Office of Technology — Email Retention Policy

Document ID: COT-GOV-006
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Office of the Chief Data Officer

## Retention Periods
- Standard employee email is retained for 7 years, then automatically deleted.
- Email belonging to executive and elected officials is retained permanently as a public record.
- Transitory messages (meeting logistics, routine notices) may be deleted by the user at any time.

## Litigation Hold
- When a litigation hold is issued, all affected mailboxes are exempt from automatic deletion until the hold is released.
- A litigation hold overrides all standard retention periods.

## Access
- Retention and hold configuration is managed centrally by the messaging team and may not be altered by individual users.
