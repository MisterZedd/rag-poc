# Cascadia Office of Technology — Security Incident Response Policy

Document ID: COT-SEC-005
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Office of the Chief Information Security Officer

## Reporting
- Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery.
- Reports are submitted through the incident portal or the 24-hour SOC hotline.

## Severity Levels
- **SEV1 (Critical):** Active breach or widespread outage. Response begins immediately; executive notification within 30 minutes.
- **SEV2 (High):** Significant impact to a single system or agency. Response begins within 1 hour.
- **SEV3 (Moderate):** Limited impact, contained. Response begins within 4 hours.
- **SEV4 (Low):** Minor or informational. Response begins within 1 business day.

## Post-Incident
- A blameless postmortem is required for all SEV1 and SEV2 incidents within 5 business days of resolution.
